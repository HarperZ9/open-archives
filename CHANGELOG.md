# Changelog

## Unreleased

open-archives finds free, reusable media across public archives and museum collections with one search, downloads the items you pick, and records the licence and rights basis of every file, so you know what you can ship.

- One query across archive.org, Wikimedia Commons, museum collections and NASA, with licence classes in the results table.
- `gather` downloads picks with size caps and writes a receipt; `derive` cuts clips, loops and textures with lineage receipts.
- `check-derived` is a clean-room gate for assets made by hand.
